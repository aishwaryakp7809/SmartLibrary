SMART LIBRARY RULES

Library Timings:
The library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM from Monday to Saturday.

Book Borrowing:
Each student can borrow up to 3 books at a time.

Borrowing Period:
Books can be borrowed for 14 days.

Renewal:
A book can be renewed once if there is no reservation from another student.

Late Return:
Students may be charged a fine for returning books after the due date.

Study Zones:
The library has General Study, Silent Study, and Discussion zones.

Silent Study Zone:
Students should maintain silence in the Silent Study Zone.

Library Membership:
Students must carry their valid college ID card when borrowing books.
